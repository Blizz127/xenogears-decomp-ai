## Host-boundary audit of `0x80025044`

The production source body exists in `src/slus_006.64/system/temp1.c` and
the linked object is 168 bytes / 42 instructions. Retail consumes
`g_GfxImageList[g_GfxCurContext]` as a linked list of guest `Image` records:

```text
record +0x00: RECT {x,y,w,h}
record +0x08: guest image-data address, or zero for ClearImage
record +0x0C: guest next-record address
```

The generic compiled body casts those guest u32 values directly to host
pointers before calling `LoadImage`/`ClearImage`. That is unsafe in the PC
port even though the natural list is empty today. The bounded fix wraps the
retail operation: known PSX RAM/KSEG1 addresses are mapped through
`PSX_ADDR`, while unknown context/head/data values are logged, counted, and
the list is retired without a raw host dereference. The wrapper preserves
the retail clear-at-exit behavior for known and rejected lists.
